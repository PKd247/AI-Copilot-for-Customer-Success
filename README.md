# AI-Copilot-for-Customer-Success
Turn messy customer feedback into renewal-risk signals and next-best actions.
An experimental AI-powered solution that helps Customer Success professionals improve customer engagement and account management. The AI suggests; the CSM decides.

Objectives
Analyse customer feedback
Identify potential customer renewal risks
Generate customer health insights
Recommend proactive engagement actions
Support customer review and renewal preparation
Business Problem

Customer Success teams often spend considerable time reviewing customer interactions, identifying risks, and preparing account updates.

This project explores how AI can simplify these activities and help teams make better-informed decisions.

Output: AI-generated insight

Sentiment: Negative
Renewal risk: High
Key drivers: Reporting failures, slow support response, competitor evaluation
Suggested actions:
Escalate the open reporting issues internally
Schedule an executive check-in within a week
Prepare a recovery plan ahead of the renewal conversation

A Customer Success Manager reviews and decides on every recommendation.
Human-AI Collaboration

This project is designed as a copilot, not an autopilot.

Planned workflow

AI surfaces signals and drafts recommendations
The CSM validates them using relationship context and judgment
Every flag includes its reasoning, so it can be challenged
No customer-facing action is taken automatically
